If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and anything you choose to write in your message.

- **What we use it for:** to read and reply to your message, and to keep a record of our correspondence.
- **Lawful basis:** our legitimate interests in responding to people who contact us and in running our business.
- **Who can see it:** only us, and the company that provides our email service, which stores messages on our behalf. We do not sell your information or share it for marketing.
- **How long we keep it:** only as long as we need to deal with your message and keep sensible business records. After that, we delete it.

Please don't include health information or other sensitive details in an email unless we need them to answer your question.
